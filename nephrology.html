Overview

Kidney disease rarely travels alone, so our nephrology service works alongside cardiology, endocrinology and urology. We manage the full range — from the first abnormal creatinine through to established dialysis — with dedicated haemodialysis stations on site.

Conditions we treat

  • Acute kidney injury (AKI)
  • Chronic kidney disease, stages 1–5
  • Diabetic & hypertensive kidney disease
  • Glomerulonephritis
  • Nephrotic & nephritic syndromes
  • Polycystic kidney disease
  • Electrolyte disorders, anaemia & mineral-bone disease
  • End-stage kidney disease requiring dialysis

Procedures & treatments

  • Medical management of CKD/AKI, with BP & diabetes optimisation
  • Haemodialysis — scheduled and urgent sessions with continuous vitals
  • Peritoneal dialysis — CAPD training, catheter care and follow-up
  • Vascular access planning
  • Pre-transplant evaluation and renal diet counselling

When to see a specialist

  • Frothy urine, or blood in the urine
  • Blood pressure that will not come under control
  • A rising creatinine on a routine blood test
  • Persistent vomiting, swelling, or recurrent kidney infections

What to expect

Our unit runs dedicated haemodialysis stations with RO-treated water and single-use disposables. Emergency dialysis is supported by the ICU and 24×7 emergency department, so urgent sessions do not require a transfer.

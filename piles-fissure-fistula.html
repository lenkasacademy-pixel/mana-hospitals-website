Overview

Piles (haemorrhoids), anal fissures and fistulas are common and very treatable — yet many people wait years out of embarrassment. Our proctology service, led by a laparoscopic and laser anorectal surgeon, offers a private consultation, a clear diagnosis and the least invasive treatment that will work, from diet and medicines to day-care laser procedures.

Conditions we treat

  • Piles (haemorrhoids) — internal and external, all grades
  • Anal fissure — acute and chronic
  • Fistula-in-ano
  • Perianal abscess
  • Pilonidal sinus
  • Rectal prolapse
  • Bleeding or itching around the anus
  • Constipation-related anal problems

Procedures & treatments

  • Laser haemorrhoidoplasty for piles
  • Laser and minimally invasive fissure treatment
  • Fistula surgery, including sphincter-saving techniques
  • Abscess drainage
  • Pilonidal sinus excision
  • Diet, stool-softener and sitz-bath programmes for early-stage disease

When to see a specialist

  • Bright red blood on the toilet paper or in the pan
  • Pain, burning or a cut-like feeling while passing stools
  • A lump or swelling near the anus, or discharge of pus
  • Symptoms that keep returning despite creams and home remedies

What to expect

The examination is short and done in private. Many early piles and fissures settle with medicines and diet changes. When a procedure is needed, laser and minimally invasive options usually mean less pain, minimal bleeding and a return to work within days.

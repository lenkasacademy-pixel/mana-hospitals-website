Overview

Ear, nose and throat problems affect sleep, hearing, speech and breathing — and in children they can affect learning too. Our ENT surgeon examines, diagnoses and treats these conditions with medicines or procedures as needed, for children and adults alike.

Conditions we treat

  • Ear infections, ear discharge and ear pain
  • Hearing loss and ear wax
  • Sinusitis and blocked nose
  • Tonsillitis and adenoids
  • Allergic rhinitis
  • Nose bleeds
  • Vertigo and giddiness
  • Hoarseness and voice problems

Procedures & treatments

  • ENT examination and endoscopy
  • Ear wax removal and ear toilet
  • Treatment plans for sinusitis and allergy
  • Tonsil and adenoid surgery (tonsillectomy, adenoidectomy)
  • Nose and sinus surgery — septoplasty, endoscopic sinus surgery, nasal polyps
  • Ear surgery — ear drum repair and other ear procedures
  • Hearing assessment referral and follow-up

When to see a specialist

  • Ear pain or discharge, especially in a child
  • Reduced hearing, ringing in the ears or giddiness
  • A blocked nose or sinus pain for more than two weeks
  • Repeated sore throats, loud snoring, or a voice change that lasts more than three weeks

What to expect

Most ENT problems are diagnosed at the first visit with a careful examination. Bring any previous reports or scans; treatment usually starts the same day.

Common questions.

Tonsil removal is considered for frequent, severe tonsillitis, breathing or sleep problems, or complications such as an abscess. Many children are managed with medicines alone.

Most sinusitis settles with medicines, nasal sprays and steam. Surgery is considered only for chronic or complicated cases.

Cotton buds can push wax deeper and injure the ear. If wax is blocking hearing, an ENT doctor can remove it safely.

Overview

Medical and surgical gastroenterology sit in the same department here, which matters: the endoscopy that makes the diagnosis and the laparoscopic surgery that treats it are planned by one team, with imaging and emergency cover in the same building.

Conditions we treat

  • Acidity / GERD, gastritis, peptic ulcer
  • Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)
  • Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)
  • Jaundice, hepatitis, fatty liver, cirrhosis
  • Pancreatitis
  • GI bleeding
  • Gallbladder & bile duct stones

Procedures & treatments

  • Upper GI endoscopy (EGD)
  • Colonoscopy
  • ERCP
  • Laparoscopic cholecystectomy
  • Laparoscopic appendectomy
  • Hernia repair — TEP / TAPP / open mesh

When to see a specialist

  • Persistent acidity, burning or trouble swallowing
  • Black or blood-stained stools, vomiting blood, or unexplained anaemia
  • Yellowing of the eyes or skin
  • Severe or recurrent abdominal pain

What to expect

For an upper GI endoscopy, fast for 6–8 hours. For a colonoscopy, follow the bowel preparation instructions and take clear liquids the day before — and arrange for someone to accompany you home.

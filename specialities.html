Detailed departments

Where we go deepest.

Eight departments with dedicated consultants, on-site procedures and their own care pathways.

General Medicine & Diabetology

Diabetes, hypertension, thyroid, fever and infections — with same-day consultation, an on-site laboratory and 24×7 emergency backup behind every appointment.

Learn more

Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement

Fractures and trauma, arthritis of the knee, hip and shoulder, ligament and sports injuries — with arthroscopy, joint replacement and physiotherapy on site.

Learn more

Nephrology & Dialysis

Complete kidney care — from early detection and medical management through to haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis, with ICU and 24×7 emergency behind it.

Learn more

Gastroenterology & Surgical Gastro

Acidity and GERD, IBS, IBD, pancreatitis, jaundice and liver disease — with diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy alongside advanced laparoscopic surgery.

Learn more

Neurology & Neurosurgery

Stroke, seizures, headache, neuropathy and movement disorders — with CT and MRI, on-site EEG and EMG–NCV, and 24×7 emergency cover.

Learn more

Pulmonology & Chest Medicine

Asthma, COPD, allergy, pneumonia, tuberculosis, interstitial lung disease and sleep apnoea — with spirometry, bronchoscopy and sleep studies.

Learn more

Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Comprehensive maternity care and women's health, from preconception to postnatal — with paediatric and anaesthesia support alongside.

Learn more

Urology & Stone Care

Kidney stones, prostate problems, urinary infections, andrology and incontinence — with laser stone treatment and minimally invasive prostate surgery.

Learn more

Piles, Fissure & Fistula (Proctology)

Bleeding, pain or a lump while passing stools? Our anorectal surgeons treat piles, fissures, fistulas and pilonidal sinus — with laser and minimally invasive options and quick recovery.

Learn more

General & Laparoscopic Surgery

Hernia, gallbladder stones, appendicitis, lumps and cysts — treated with keyhole (laparoscopic) surgery wherever possible, for smaller scars and a faster return home.

Learn more

Paediatrics & Child Care

Fevers, coughs, vaccinations, growth and nutrition — care for newborns, children and teenagers, with NICU support and 24×7 emergency in the same building.

Learn more

24×7 Emergency & Critical Care

Accidents, chest pain, breathlessness, stroke, poisoning or high fever — our emergency department, ICU and NICU are staffed around the clock, every day of the year.

Learn more

ENT — Ear, Nose & Throat

Ear pain, hearing problems, sinusitis, tonsils, snoring and voice problems — diagnosed and treated, including ENT surgery, by an ENT surgeon for adults and children.

Learn more

Radiology & Diagnostics

CT, MRI, ultrasound, X-ray, Doppler and laboratory tests in the same building as your doctor — reported by a radiologist, with most results the same day.

Learn more

Infertility & Fertility Care

Trying to conceive without success? Step-by-step evaluation and treatment for couples, led by a gynaecologist with a fellowship in reproductive medicine.

Learn more

Diabetes & Thyroid Care (Diabetology)

Sugar control, insulin, thyroid and cholesterol — personalised plans from diabetologists, with same-day lab tests and regular follow-up.

Learn more

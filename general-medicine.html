Overview

General Medicine is the front door to the hospital. Our physicians diagnose and manage the everyday conditions that affect adults — and, because the lab, pharmacy and ICU are all in the same building, escalate immediately when something turns out to be more serious.

Conditions we treat

  • Diabetes & blood sugar control
  • Hypertension / high blood pressure
  • Fever & infections
  • Thyroid disorders
  • Cholesterol management
  • Anaemia & weakness
  • Respiratory complaints & cough
  • Digestive problems
  • Allergies & skin issues
  • Urinary tract infection
  • Vitamin deficiency
  • Health screening & check-ups

Procedures & treatments

  • Same-day consultation
  • On-site lab & diagnostics
  • Chronic disease management programmes
  • 24×7 emergency backup
  • In-house pharmacy

When to see a specialist

  • A fever that has not settled in three days
  • Newly diagnosed or poorly controlled diabetes or blood pressure
  • Persistent fatigue, weight change or breathlessness
  • An annual check-up, or before starting long-term medication

What to expect

Bring any previous prescriptions, medical reports or test results you have. Most blood work — sugar, thyroid function, cholesterol, CBC — is run on site with same-day results, so treatment usually begins at the same visit.
